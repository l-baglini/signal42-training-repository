# Summer Playtime — The Summer Build

> Session 7 · Coders Track · the last one before the break.

This is the optional challenge to take with you over the summer, closing out
Track A (AI-Assisted Coding) before Track B (the building track) starts in the
fourth week of August. That first session back is the **showcase**.

## The challenge

Build something you actually care about — the way these six sessions taught you.

- **Your idea, any area** — your own itch, a passion project, whatever pulls you.
- **Any language, any stack, any size** — a weekend hack or something you nurse
  all summer.
- **Your own time, your own access** — completely optional, nothing here is
  expected of you.

Bring it back for the first session in August. Bring something, or bring
nothing. No pressure.

## Build it with the craft

Your repo should carry the craft from Track A, not just the finished thing:

- **A spec that directed it** — the intent the agent built to.
- **A review pass on the AI's work** — you owned the check.
- **A `CLAUDE.md` or `AGENTS.md`** — so a fresh model could pick it up tomorrow.

You're recognised for all of it — the skills you've built, not just the output.
Make the thinking travel with the code.

## Go further (optional)

- **Put AI inside it** — an agent, an MCP tool, an LLM feature. Do this and you
  walk into the building track having already touched B1.
- **Go off-piste** — try open-source models, a different provider, whatever you
  fancy. No rules here at all.

## Stuck for an idea?

A deliberately wide net:

- One-shot a whole working app from a single spec.
- Automate the most repetitive 40% of your week.
- An AI data-scientist sidekick that explores, charts and explains a dataset.
- A small game with one original mechanic.
- Learn a new stack you've been curious about.
- Runtime AI done right — a model in the product, with citations, guardrails and
  a visible cost.

The strongest builds last time had a **clear user** and a **two-minute demo**.
Aim for that.

## The awards — four ways to win

- 🏆 **Most Technically Advanced** — real engineering under the hood.
- 🏆 **Most Visually Engaging Front End** — looks and feels brilliant.
- 🏆 **Most Advanced Agentic Capability** — AI that plans and acts on its own.
- 🏆 **Most Unexpected Idea** — the build nobody saw coming.

## How it lands

- **Show & tell** — every build gets shown at the first session back. A showcase,
  never a scoreboard.
- **One award for each build** — a category for every kind of maker.
- **Learning notes** — what was strong, what to borrow, what to try next, for
  everyone who enters.

It's voluntary, on your own time. Where you publish, leadership will confirm —
and there may be a prize on top. **Keep anything you share safe: no secrets in
the code.**
